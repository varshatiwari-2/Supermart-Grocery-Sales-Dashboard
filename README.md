### **Supermart Grocery Sales \& Retail Analytics Dashboard**



An interactive management-facing Excel dashboard analyzing retail sales performance, product profitability, discount dynamics, and geographic distribution across Tamil Nadu, India.



**Project Context**

This project converts a 9,994-record retail dataset into an executive dashboard designed to help management monitor key performance metrics, track margins, and evaluate product and regional growth opportunities without sifting through raw data.



**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

Total Sales: ₹14,956,982.00

Total Profit: ₹3,747,121.20

Total Orders: 9,994

Overall Profit Margin: 25.05%

Average Discount Rate: 15.65%



**Key Business Insights**

1.Category Contributions: All 7 product categories maintain high profitability (\~24.4%–25.4% margin), with Snacks (₹22.37L Sales, ₹5.68L Profit) and Eggs, Meat \& Fish (₹22.67L Sales, ₹5.67L Profit) leading overall volume.

2.Top Sub-Categories: Health Drinks (₹10.51L) and Soft Drinks (₹10.33L) are the highest revenue-generating product sub-categories.

3.Geographic Distribution: The West Region leads sales with ₹47.98L (32%), followed by East (₹42.48L, 28%), Central (₹34.68L, 23%), and South (₹24.40L, 16%). Top cities include Kanyakumari, Vellore, and Bodi.

4.Discount Dynamics: Profitability remains strong across discount ranges (\~0.10–0.35), indicating efficient retail pricing strategies\*\*.\*\*



**Workbook Architecture**

Dashboard — Executive-facing layout featuring high-level KPIs, Category Sales vs. Profit Bar Charts, and Regional Share Pie Charts.

Analysis — Calculation engine using dynamic Excel formulas (SUMIF, AVERAGEIF) to aggregate performance by Category, Sub-Category, and Region.

Data — Cleaned raw dataset containing 9,994 order records.
